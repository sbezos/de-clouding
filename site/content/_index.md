## The short version

The big AI models were trained on everyone's photos, writing, code and conversations — things nobody shared for that purpose. That's done now, and some of the result is being handed back as free, open models anyone can run. What *isn't* handed back is everything you keep feeding in: your prompts, your documents, your habits, your home.

This site is about closing that loop on your side. Keep the useful parts. Keep your data. You don't need to be an engineer, and you don't need an expensive machine — most of the wins here cost nothing.

## How it works

1. **Pick a service** you'd like to own instead of rent (photos, mail, your smart home, your AI assistant…).
2. **Pick a budget tier**, from "$0, use what I have" up to "I've got the bug."
3. **Copy the prompt** at the bottom of the page into Claude or another assistant and let it walk you through the move, one step at a time, with the safety rails already written in.

That's it. Start with whichever one annoys you most.
