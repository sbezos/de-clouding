Start with **Backups** — it's the safety net under everything else. After that, pick whichever bothers you most. Already decided you want a small home server? [Build the box in an afternoon](/box/) has the shopping list and a copy-paste kit for every room.


{{% deep title="In what order, and why" %}}
The guides have a dependency graph, not just a list:

- **Backups** first, because every other move creates a copy you now have to protect, and because the off-site bucket you set up here is the model for "cloud you control" used everywhere else.
- **Network & remote access** second, because Tailscale is the way you'll reach everything else from your phone, and because the VLAN for gadgets is what makes cheap cameras and smart plugs safe to own.
- **Passwords & notes** early, because it's an afternoon, it's the master key, and the win is immediate.
- **Photos & files** when you have a weekend and the drive; it's the biggest data move and benefits from the first two being done.
- **Email** is independent and can happen any time — it's a Tier 0 move with no hardware.
- **Smart home, Media, Cameras, Games** are parallel; each needs the network guide's isolation to be done right.
- **AI assistant** last or whenever you like; it depends on nothing but gains from the network guide (phone access) and from Photos (searching your own library).
