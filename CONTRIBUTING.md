# Contributing

Text is CC BY-SA 4.0. Corrections and new guides welcome.

- Open an issue for anything wrong, out of date, or missing.
- PRs: guides live in `site/content/guides/`; keep the page template (what you gain / what you still rent / tiers strip / hand-off prompt) and the tone — kind, no hardware assumptions, every budget tier counts, facts about services not motives.
- No affiliate links, no analytics, no backend. Ever.
